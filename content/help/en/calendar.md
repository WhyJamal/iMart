# Production Calendar

Working and non-working days for the year are defined in this section — it serves as the basis for calculations in [Work Schedules](/help/work-schedules) and the [Timesheet](/help/timesheets).

## Selecting a year

The desired year is selected from the panel at the top of the page. A calendar is created separately for each year.

## Marking days

Using the calendar grid, each day is marked as a working or non-working day.

## Why it matters

Work schedules and timesheets rely on this calendar to calculate an employee's actual working days.
