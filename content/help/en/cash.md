# Cash

All cash register and bank account movements are tracked in this section.

## Balances

The top of the page shows the current cash and bank balances. These are updated automatically from [Sales](/help/sales) and other documents, as well as by manually entered movements.

## New movement

"New movement" lets you record a direction (income/expense), point, amount, method (cash/card/QR), and a note.

## Transferring between locations

A separate "Transfer" dialog is used to move cash from one location to another.

## Filtering and the list

The movement list can be filtered by point and date range. Each row shows the type, point, method, amount, note, and date.
