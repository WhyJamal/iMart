# Counterparties

Suppliers and individual buyers are managed in this list.

## List

Each row shows the name, tax ID, type (supplier or buyer), phone number, number of purchases, and current debt.

## Paying off debt

A payment to a counterparty with debt is entered right from the row — by cash, card, or QR. Payment history can be viewed in a separate window.

## Related sections

[Purchases](/help/purchases) from suppliers are linked to the corresponding counterparty. The total debt across all counterparties is summarized in the [Debt Report](/help/debt-report).
