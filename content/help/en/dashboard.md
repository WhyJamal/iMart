# Dashboard

The main page shown after logging in — the current state of the business is visible at a glance.

## Metrics

The top section shows: today's sales, monthly sales, monthly purchase total, cash and bank balances, and the number of low-stock products.

## Charts

Below are charts for the sales trend by day, a monthly overview, the payment method breakdown, top-selling products, and revenue by location.

All figures are calculated automatically from documents in the [Sales](/help/sales), [Purchases](/help/purchases), and [Cash](/help/cash) sections.
