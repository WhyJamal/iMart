# Debt Report

A consolidated overview of all debts — in both directions: what's owed to us, and what we owe to suppliers.

## Owed to us

A list of customers with debt from the [Clients (debtors)](/help/debtors) section, with the total amount.

## We owe

A list of suppliers with debt from among [Counterparties](/help/contragents), with the total amount.

Both lists are sorted from the largest debt down.
