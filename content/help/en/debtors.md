# Clients (debtors)

This section is used to sell to customers on credit and track their current debt.

## How a debt is created

When "On credit" is selected as the payment method at checkout, the corresponding customer (debtor) is selected from the list. The full sale amount is recorded against that customer's debt.

## Debtor list

The "Clients (debtors)" page shows all debtors and their current outstanding amount. Each debtor's card opens their full payment history.

## Recording a payment

After selecting a debtor, a partial or full amount is entered through the "Record Payment" window. The entered amount is automatically subtracted from the debtor's total debt.

## Payment from the counterparties list

Partial payments to suppliers (counterparties) work the same way, through the corresponding row in the counterparties list.

## Debt report

A summary of all debts is available under *Reports → Debt Report*.
