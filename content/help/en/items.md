# Products

The organization's overall product catalog is managed in this section.

## Creating a product

Creating a product requires a name, code, category, unit of measure, standard price, and an image.

## Search and list

The list can be searched by name or code. Each row shows the image, name, code, category, unit, and price.

## Related sections

Catalog products are selected in [Stock Intake](/help/stock-intake), [Purchases](/help/purchases), [POS](/help/pos), and nearly every other document. A [Promotion](/help/promotions) on a product is also set based on this catalog.
