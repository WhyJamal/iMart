# Payroll

Wages owed to employees are calculated as a document in this section.

## New document

A location is selected; the calculation is based on the rate from [Employee Salary](/help/salary) and the actual worked days from the [Timesheet](/help/timesheets).

## List

The list shows payroll documents by location and period.

## Why it matters

Calculated payroll is accounted for as an expense in the [Profit & Loss](/help/profit-loss) report.
