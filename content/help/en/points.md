# Locations

Each of the organization's store locations (branches) is registered in this section.

## List

Each row shows the location's name, the number of [warehouses](/help/warehouses) it has, and the number of assigned staff.

## Adding a new location

Creating a location requires a name. Once created, a warehouse and [users](/help/users) can be assigned to it.

## Why it matters

Nearly every document ([POS](/help/pos), [Stock Intake](/help/stock-intake), [Purchases](/help/purchases), and others) is recorded against a specific location — reports are also filtered this way.
