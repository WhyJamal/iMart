# POS (Checkout)

The checkout screen is where you sell products, take payment and print a receipt.

## Adding products

- Add a product to the cart by **scanning its barcode** or **searching** for it.
- Adjust quantity with the `+` / `-` buttons.

## Manually adjusting the price

When needed — for example, to apply an individual discount for a customer — the line total for a product in the cart can be changed directly: click the amount on the right (shown with a dotted underline) and enter the new value. The entered amount already includes tax, no further calculation is required.

## Promotions

If a product has an active promotion (discount), the price drops automatically when added to the cart — the old price is shown crossed out, with the new price and discount percentage next to it.

## Payment

From the payment panel at the bottom:

- **Cash** or **Card** — enter the full amount, the sale completes immediately.
- **On credit** — pick a customer (debtor) from the list; the amount is added to their debt, and you can record a payment later from the [Clients (debtors)](/help/debtors) section.

## Receipt

After a sale completes, click "Receipt" in the confirmation screen to print it. The receipt template (which columns show, header/footer text) is configured under *[Settings](/help/settings) → Receipt Template*.
