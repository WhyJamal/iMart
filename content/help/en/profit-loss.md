# Profit & Loss

The organization's financial result for a selected period — accounting for revenue, cost of goods, and expenses.

## Filters

Data can be filtered by date range and location; a print button is also available.

## Structure

- **Revenue**: revenue from [sales](/help/sales) minus returns, giving net revenue.
- **Cost of goods**: the cost of goods sold is subtracted, giving gross profit.
- **Expenses**: the [write-off](/help/write-offs) amount, manually entered expenses, and [payroll](/help/payroll) are added.
- The result shows net profit and margin (as a percentage).
