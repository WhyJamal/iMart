# Promotions

Temporary discounts on products are set up in this section.

## Creating a promotion

A location, warehouse, and cell are selected; a promotion name, discount percentage, and end date are entered. One or more products are then added to the promotion.

## How it works

When a product with an active promotion is added at [POS](/help/pos), the price drops automatically — the old price is shown crossed out, with the new price and percentage next to it.

## Expiration

Once the specified end date passes, the promotion is automatically deactivated and the product sells at its standard price.
