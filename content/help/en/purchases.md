# Purchases

Documents for buying products from a supplier are managed in this section — unlike a [Stock Intake](/help/stock-intake) document, a purchase is linked to a specific [counterparty](/help/contragents).

## Creating a purchase

When creating a document, a point, warehouse, and supplier (counterparty) are selected. Each row specifies a product from the existing catalog, quantity, and cost.

## List

The list shows the purchase number, counterparty, item count, total amount, and date. Expanding a row reveals the line-item breakdown: product, code, quantity, unit price, and line total.

## Related sections

A purchase increases warehouse stock and, if payment is deferred, updates the counterparty's debt. To return defective or unsuitable goods, use the [Supplier Returns](/help/purchase-returns) section.
