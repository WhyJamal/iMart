# Sales Returns

Registering a product returned by a customer is done in this section.

## Creating a return

The search is done by the sale number. For each product in the found sale, the quantity sold, returnable, quantity being returned, and price are shown.

## Reason and refund

A reason for the return is entered, and a refund method (cash/card/QR) is selected.

## Result

Warehouse stock increases by the returned quantity, and the corresponding amount is refunded to the customer.
