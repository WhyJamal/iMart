# Sales

The "Sales" page lists all completed sales.

## List

Each row shows the sale number, item count, total amount, and date. If a sale was made on credit, a corresponding badge appears next to the amount.

## Viewing details

Clicking a row expands a table with the sale's line items — product name, code, quantity, unit price, and line total.

## Printing a receipt

The printer icon at the end of the row reopens the sale's receipt and lets you print it.

## Deleting a sale

The trash icon deletes a sale — the confirmation dialog shows the sale number. Deleting a sale automatically restores the corresponding warehouse stock and reverses any related cash movements.
