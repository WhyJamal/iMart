# Settings

Parameters for the organization's overall operating mode are set in this section.

## Pricing mode

One of two methods is chosen: selling at the price set in the catalog, or at the average cost of goods received into the warehouse.

## Tax percentage

The tax percentage calculated at [POS](/help/pos) is entered here.

## Receipt template

The "Receipt Template" link opens a separate page where which columns appear on the receipt, as well as the header and footer text, are configured.
