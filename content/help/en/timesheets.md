# Timesheet

Employees' actual worked days are recorded as a document per location.

## New timesheet

A location and period are selected; the document is built from [Work Schedules](/help/work-schedules) and the [Production Calendar](/help/calendar).

## List

The list shows each timesheet document by location and period.

## Why it matters

An approved timesheet serves as the basis for the [Payroll](/help/payroll) document.
