# Product Transfers

Moving product stock from one location/warehouse/cell to another is done in this section.

## Creating a transfer

A location, warehouse, and cell are selected separately for the source (from) and destination (to). The available stock in the source cell is shown automatically, and the product and quantity are selected from it.

## Result

Once confirmed, the stock in the source cell decreases and the destination cell's stock increases by the same amount.
