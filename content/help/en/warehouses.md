# Warehouse

Warehouses belonging to each [location](/help/points), and the cells within them, are managed in this section.

## Creating a warehouse

Creating a warehouse requires a location and a name. A single warehouse can contain several cells — stock is tracked at the cell level.

## List

The list shows each warehouse's location, name, and number of cells.

## Related sections

Warehouses and cells are selected in [Stock Intake](/help/stock-intake), [Purchases](/help/purchases), [Transfers](/help/transfers), and [Write-offs](/help/write-offs) documents. A consolidated view of stock is available in the [Material Report](/help/material-report).
