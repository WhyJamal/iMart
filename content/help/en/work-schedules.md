# Work Schedules

Employee shift patterns are set up in this section.

## Templates

A reusable template is created first — for example, a "5/2" or "rotating shift" pattern.

## Schedule

Based on the template and the [Production Calendar](/help/calendar), a schedule is built for a specific period. The schedule is then assigned to an employee in the [Users](/help/users) section.

## Relation to other sections

The resulting schedule serves as the basis for the [Timesheet](/help/timesheets) and [Payroll](/help/payroll) documents.
