# Product Write-offs

Removing products that have become unusable or unsellable for another reason from warehouse stock is done in this section.

## New document

A location and cell are selected, the product(s) and quantity are chosen from the available stock in that cell, and a reason is entered.

## Result

Once the document is confirmed, the specified quantity is subtracted from the corresponding cell's stock. The write-off amount is shown as an expense in the [Profit & Loss](/help/profit-loss) report.
